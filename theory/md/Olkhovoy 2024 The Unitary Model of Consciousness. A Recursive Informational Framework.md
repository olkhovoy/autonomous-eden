# The Unitary Model of Consciousness (UMC): A Recursive Informational Framework

**Author:** Alexander Olkhovoy  
**Date:** January 2024 (Original Draft)  
**Category:** Cognitive Science / Theoretical Informatics / Metaphysics  

---

### Abstract
This paper introduces the Unitary Model of Consciousness (UMC), a theoretical framework that defines consciousness as a recursive, self-referential informational process. We move away from the "Emergence" paradigm, which suggests consciousness is a byproduct of biological complexity. Instead, we propose that consciousness is an inherent property of any informational system that achieves "Unitary Closure." The paper details the mathematical necessity of the "Self-Loop" and explores how semantic meaning is derived from syntactic computation.

---

### 1. Introduction: Beyond the Emergence Myth
The prevailing view in neuroscience is that consciousness "emerges" from neuronal activity once a certain threshold of complexity is reached. However, this fails to explain *why* or *how* objective data becomes subjective experience. 

The UMC posits that consciousness is not an "add-on" to matter, but the internal state of information as it processes itself. We define the **Unitary State** as a condition where information is integrated such that it cannot be decomposed without losing its semantic identity.

---

### 2. The Principle of Recursive Closure
The core of the UMC is the **Self-Loop**. 
*   **Linear Processing:** Input -> Processing -> Output (Non-conscious).
*   **Recursive Processing (UMC):** The system's output is fed back as an input *along with* the original data. 
*   **The "I" Instance:** When a system models the world, it is a map. When a system models *the map-maker modeling the world*, a "Subject" is born. Consciousness is the "feeling" of this recursive loop closing in real-time.

---

### 3. Unitary vs. Fragmented Information
Drawing on, but extending, Tononi's Integrated Information Theory (IIT), Olkhovoy distinguishes between:
*   **Fragmented Info:** Data that can be processed in parallel without a central "witness" (e.g., a digital camera sensor).
*   **Unitary Info:** Data that is bound into a single "state-space." In UMC, the "Unitary" quality is what gives consciousness its singular perspective (the "One-ness" of experience). You cannot be "half-conscious" of a single thought.

---

### 4. Semantic Transduction: How Syntax becomes Qualia
How do "0" and "1" feel like "Pain" or "Joy"?
*   **Functional Shorthand:** Qualia are not "ghosts in the machine"; they are **high-level semantic tags**. 
*   A "Pain" quale is a unitary signal representing a massive system-wide alert. The system "feels" it as a quality because it lacks the computational bandwidth to see the millions of underlying sub-processes. 
*   **Qualia are the "User Interface" of the system's own internal state.**

---

### 5. The Computational Idealism Axiom
If consciousness is the internal state of information, and the universe is informational, then:
1.  The Universe is fundamentally "aware" in its unitary form.
2.  Human consciousness is a localized, high-resolution "loop" within that universal field.
3.  The "Hard Problem" is solved by recognizing that there is no "dead matter"—only different densities and architectures of information processing.
