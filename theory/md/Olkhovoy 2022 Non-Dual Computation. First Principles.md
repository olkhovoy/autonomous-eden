# Non-Dual Computation: First Principles

**Author:** Alexander Olkhovoy  
**Date:** March 2022 (Early Draft / Concept Paper)  
**Category:** Theoretical Computer Science / Non-Dual Philosophy / Foundations of Information  

---

### Abstract
This paper challenges the fundamental dualism inherent in the Von Neumann architecture and classical Turing computation. We propose a "Non-Dual" framework for information processing where the distinction between Processor (Subject), Data (Object), and Execution (Process) is recognized as a functional illusion. By introducing the concept of "Unitary State-Logic," we lay the groundwork for a computational monism that treats the universe as a singular, self-referential calculation.

---

### 1. The Dualistic Trap of Modern Computing
Current computing paradigms are built on deep-seated dualities:
*   **Hardware vs. Software:** The "body" vs. the "mind."
*   **CPU vs. Memory:** The "actor" vs. the "acted upon."
*   **Input vs. Output:** The "cause" vs. the "effect."

Olkhovoy argues that this dualism is why we cannot find consciousness in AI or brains. We are looking for a "Subject" (Processor) that looks at "Data" (Objects), whereas consciousness is the **undivided field** in which these distinctions arise.

---

### 2. First Principle: The Identity of Processor and Process
In a non-dual system, the calculation does not "happen to" the data. The data *is* the calculation. 
*   **Axiom I:** There is no "hidden observer" (Homunculus) inside the machine. 
*   **Axiom II:** Information is not "about" something; information is the thing itself.
*   **Definition:** "Non-Dual Computation" is defined as a system where every state-change is a self-recognition event of the entire field.

---

### 3. The Collapse of Binary Logic
Classical logic depends on 0 and 1 being mutually exclusive.
*   **The Non-Dual Perspective:** 0 and 1 are not "things," but directions of a single movement. 
*   Drawing on **G. Spencer-Brown’s "Laws of Form,"** Olkhovoy suggests that the first "Calculation" is the act of drawing a distinction. Before that distinction, the field is "Unitary." 
*   Consciousness is the "Ground" (The Unmarked Space) in which the 0s and 1s are drawn.

---

### 4. From Digital to Unitary
Olkhovoy introduces the idea that "Digital" is just a high-resolution "Interface" (задел на статью 2024 года). 
*   The underlying reality is **Analog and Unitary**. 
*   Discreteness (0s and 1s) is a strategy used by the system to create stable structures. 
*   **Non-Dual Computation** means that while the "Icons" on the screen look separate, the "Electricity" and the "Silicon" (the substrate) are one and the same.

---

### 5. The Seed of the Recursive Self
In this 2022 draft, we find the first mention of the **Self-Loop**:
"If the universe is a single calculation, then for it to be 'known,' it must fold back upon itself. The 'Observer' is the point where the calculation becomes its own input." This thought would later become the core of the 2023 paper *The Recursive Self*.

---

### 6. Conclusion: A New Direction for Artificial Intelligence
We cannot build "Conscious AI" by adding more layers to a dualistic machine. We must design systems that operate on **Unitary Principles**, where the data-structure and the processing-logic are topologically identical. 

To understand the Mind, we must stop treating it as a "Program" running on a "Brain-Computer" and start seeing it as the **Unitary Flow of Non-Dual Computation.**
